# ✨ CustomerPulse AI

### AI-Powered Customer Intelligence Platform

CustomerPulse AI is an intelligent customer analytics platform designed to help businesses identify customers at risk of churn before they leave.

The platform analyzes important customer signals and transforms them into actionable insights, helping businesses take proactive steps to improve customer retention.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Businesses often lose customers without receiving early warnings.

Traditional dashboards show customer data but do not clearly explain:

- Which customers are likely to churn
- Why they are at risk
- What actions should be taken
- How different actions could reduce churn risk

CustomerPulse AI solves this problem by turning customer signals into intelligent, actionable recommendations.

---

## 🤖 How CustomerPulse AI Works

CustomerPulse AI follows an intelligent decision-making workflow:

**Customer Signals → Risk Analysis → Explainable Insights → Recommended Action → Retention Strategy → Impact Simulation**

The platform analyzes:

- 📅 Days since last login
- 📊 Product engagement
- 🎫 Open support tickets
- 😊 Customer sentiment
- ⏳ Subscription renewal timeline
- 💰 Monthly revenue

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔴 AI Customer Risk Analyzer

Enter customer signals and receive a churn risk assessment.

The system identifies whether a customer is:

- High Risk
- Medium Risk
- Healthy

---

### 🧠 Explainable AI Score Breakdown

CustomerPulse AI explains why a customer received a particular risk score.

For example:

- Reduced recent activity
- Low product engagement
- Multiple unresolved support tickets
- Negative customer sentiment
- Approaching subscription renewal

This makes the risk assessment transparent and actionable.

---

### ⚡ Recommended Next Best Action

Instead of only detecting risk, CustomerPulse AI recommends what the business should do next.

Examples include:

- Contacting the customer immediately
- Resolving support issues
- Scheduling a personalized success call
- Improving customer engagement

---

### 💜 AI Retention Strategy

The platform generates a personalized recovery plan for at-risk customers.

The strategy can include:

1. Immediate intervention
2. Resolving customer issues
3. Personalized success calls
4. Engagement recovery plans

---

### 🔮 What-If Risk Simulator

CustomerPulse AI allows teams to simulate the potential impact of customer improvements.

Users can test actions such as:

- Resolving support issues
- Increasing product engagement
- Improving customer sentiment
- Increasing login activity

The platform then predicts how those improvements could reduce churn risk.
